How it works

Where do students come from?

The list is synced with the Team Manager: every account with the Student role appears here. Change roles in Team Manager, not here.

How do I record a fee payment?

Click the ₹ icon on a student, enter amount, mode and note. Each payment is saved in history and appears on the bill. Use checkboxes for bulk payments.

How do I print or save a bill?

Click the receipt icon, then Print / Save PDF and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.

Why is the QR missing on a bill?

The QR shows only when a balance is pending and a QR is uploaded in Branding. Fully paid bills get a PAID stamp instead.

What does "Clear" do?

It removes only the fee, class and plan record from this page. The student's account is never touched.

How do plans work?

Create plans under Plans, assign them per student. Premium locks stay off until ENFORCE = true in stscript.js; wrap any feature in can('feature', record).
