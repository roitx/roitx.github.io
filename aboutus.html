2.5 Community Uploads & Your Data

Students can voluntarily contribute their own notes and formula sheets to PM Roitx. This is how your data is handled when you do:

In short: Aap apna note dekar dusre students ki madad kar sakte ho. Aapka naam, email ya account ID kisi note ke saath public nahi dikhaya jata, aur approval se pehle aapka note sirf aapko aur review karne wale admin/team ko dikhta hai.
  • Login required: Only logged-in users can upload. We store a private account ID with each upload so we can limit one note per chapter for each user, review abuse and handle removal requests. This ID is never shown publicly.
  • Review before publishing: A new upload is marked "Approval pending" and is visible only to you and our admin team. If it is rejected, the file and its record are permanently deleted.
  • Your identity stays private: After approval only the note itself (class, subject, chapter name and file) is shown on the site. Your name, email or profile is not displayed with it.
  • Scanning happens on your device: When you use the built-in scanner, cropping, filters and cleaning run inside your own browser. Only the final PDF is uploaded when you press Upload; your original camera photos are not sent to us.
  • Limited access: Uploads are stored on Supabase with Row Level Security, so other users cannot browse unapproved uploads or anyone's account details. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • Keep personal details out of your notes: Approved notes can be seen by all students. Please do not include phone numbers, addresses, ID cards or other private information in the pages you upload (the scanner's "Clean" tool can erase marks).
  • Remove anytime: To delete a note you uploaded, email legal@roitx.dpdns.org with the class, subject and chapter, and we will remove it promptly.

No online service can promise absolute security, but we keep access restricted and use reasonable safeguards to protect what you share with us.

2.6 User Compliance Rights

  • Right to Rectification: Update inaccurate personal profiles easily.
