📖 Complete Function & Keys Guide

1. Basic Controls & Memory
• SHIFT: Top-left yellow functions activate karta hai.
• ALPHA: Variables (A-F, X, Y, M) aur RNG key ko activate karta hai.
• STO (Store): Value ko variable me save karein.
• RCL (Recall): Stored variable ki value screen par lane ke liye.
• M+ / M-: Screen value ko Memory (M) me add ya subtract karne ke liye.

2. Trigonometry
• sin, cos, tan: Normal angle ratios nikalne ke liye.
• DEG / RAD: Top screen par click karke Degree aur Radian mode toggle karein.

3. Inverse Trigonometry
• sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹: SHIFT + sin/cos/tan press karein.

4. Hyperbolic Functions
• sinh, cosh, tanh: Pehle hyp press karein, fir sin/cos/tan dabayein.

5. Powers, Roots & Fractions
• x², x³: Square aur Cube values calculate karein.
• √, ∛: Square root aur Cube root.
• ^ (Pow): Custom power ke liye.

6. Logarithm & Exponents
• log / ln: Base-10 aur Natural logarithm.

7. Calculus, Sum & Solve
• ∫(f, a, b)dx: Definite integral, variable X. Jaise ∫(X², 0, 3)dx. Limits na do to indefinite integral milta hai: ∫(5)dx = 5x + C.
• d(f, a)/dx: SHIFT + ∫. Derivative at point a, jaise d(X², 3)/dx. Point na do to symbolic derivative: d(5X²)/dx = 10x (symbolic answers radian me hote hain).
• Σ(f, a, b) / Π(f, a, b): Sum aur Product, variable X.
• CALC: Expression me A-F, X, Y, M ho to unki values puchta hai.
• SOLVE: SHIFT + CALC (ya "=" wali equation par = dabayein). Real roots deta hai.

8. Special Keys
• (−) / ∠: Negative sign / polar form (r∠θ). ° ′ ″: Baar baar dabane par degree, minute, second.
• FACT: Prime factors. ÷R: Quotient aur remainder. PreAns: Pichla answer.
• ENG: Engineering notation. S⇔D: Fraction ↔ decimal. i: Complex number.
• Pol( / Rec(: Polar ↔ rectangular (X aur Y me store). Ceil / Intg / Round: Rounding.
• RanInt(a,b): Random integer. Rnd: Ran# (0.000-0.999). ⁿ√(n, a): n-th root.
• ab/c: Mixed fraction, jaise 2⌟1⌟3. STO / RCL: Pehle STO phir variable key (ALPHA wali).

9. Menus (Part 2)
• CONST: 30 physical constants, search karke insert karein.
• CONV: Unit conversion (length, mass, temperature…), result calculator me insert hota hai.
• MATRIX: MatA, MatB, MatC (4×4 tak). MatA × MatB, Det(MatA), Inv(MatA), Trn(MatA), Ident(3).
• CMPLX: Abs, Arg, Conj, Re, Im, aur r∠θ ↔ a+bi conversion.
• MODE: Degree/Radian, Norm/Fix/Sci (digits), fraction style. Settings save rehti hain.
• CLR / SHIFT + AC: Memory, variables, matrices, history ya sab kuch clear.
• SHIFT + DEL (INS): Overwrite mode on/off.

10. Algebra
• x! (Factorial): SHIFT + x².
• gcd / lcm: Greatest Common Divisor aur Least Common Multiple.

About ROITX Scientific Calculator Pro

Online scientific calculator with AI math solver

ROITX Scientific Calculator Pro works like a Casio-style scientific calculator in your browser. It covers trigonometry, inverse and hyperbolic functions, logarithms, powers and roots, definite integrals, derivatives, sums, products, equation solving, matrices, complex numbers, fractions and unit conversion. It is useful for school and college students, including Class 11 and Class 12 maths and physics.

Highlights

  • AI Math Solver: type, draw or scan an equation and get step-by-step answers in English or Hindi.
  • Tools panel: graphing with roots and turning points, statistics and regression, formula sheet, number base converter and molar mass calculator.
  • Four themes, live result preview, keyboard support and saved history.

Frequently asked questions

What can ROITX Scientific Calculator Pro do?

It handles trigonometry, logarithms, powers and roots, definite integrals, derivatives, sums and products, equation solving, matrices up to 4x4, complex numbers, fractions, unit conversion and 30 physical constants. The Tools panel adds graphing, statistics, a formula sheet and a molar mass calculator.

How does the AI Math Solver work?

Tap the AI key, then type an equation, draw it on the pad or scan a photo. You get a step-by-step solution in English or Hindi. Guests get one free text solve; logging in unlocks unlimited text solves plus the Draw Pad and Scan modes.

Can I type with my computer keyboard?

Yes. Digits, operators, brackets, Enter for equals, Backspace for delete, Esc to clear and the arrow keys all work.

Does it remember my settings?

Your theme, angle mode, display format, variables, matrices and the last 20 calculations are saved in your own browser.
