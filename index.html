Privacy Policy

Antim badlav: 27 November 2026

Ek nazar me: Aapka khata aapka hai. Hum aapka data bechte nahi, aapko ads nahi dikhate aur koi tracking ya analytics nahi chalate. Data sirf app chalane ke liye use hota hai.

Ads nahiData bechte nahiPIN hash me saveBackup aapke haath me

1Ye app kya hai

Aasaan Hisaab ek AI khata (ledger) app hai: jama-kharcha, udhar, budget aur reports ke liye. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaun sa data lete hain, kahan rakhte hain aur aapke paas kya control hai.

2Hum kaun sa data lete hain

  • Account: aapka mobile number, 4-digit PIN (sirf hash ke roop me), aur security question ka jawab (ye bhi hash me).
  • Khata data: aapki entries (amount, note, category, Cash/Bank/Udhar, tarikh), udhar wali parties ke naam aur balance, aur aapke budget.
  • AI Munim: jo text aap likhte ya bolte hain, bill ki photo, aur sawal poochne par aapke khate ka chhota saaransh (is period ka jama-kharcha, party balance, haal ki kuch entries).
  • Aapki pasand: theme, rang, bhasha, balance chhupana, har-mahine ki entries ki list. Ye sirf aapke phone me rehti hain.
Hum ye nahi lete: aapki location, contacts, SMS, bank ka login ya card number, aur advertising ID.

3Data kahan save hota hai

  • Aapke phone ya browser me: khata ki ek copy rehti hai, isliye internet na ho tab bhi app chalta hai.
  • Cloud (Google Firebase Firestore): aapke mobile number ke account ke andar entries sync hoti hain, taaki naye phone par bhi khata mile. Net na ho to entry phone me rukti hai aur net aate hi upload hoti hai.
  • AI request: aapka text ya photo hamare Cloudflare Worker (proxy) se hote hue Groq ke AI model tak jaata hai. Proxy ka kaam sirf request aage bhejna hai aur hamari API key chhupa kar rakhna. Hamare code me AI messages ko alag se save karne ka koi hissa nahi hai.

4Data ka upyog kisliye

  • Aapka khata dikhane, sync karne aur reports/PDF/CSV banane ke liye.
  • AI se entry samajhne, sawalon ke jawab dene aur bill padhne ke liye.
  • Login, PIN reset aur security ke liye.

Hum aapka data bechte nahi, kisi ko kiraye par nahi dete, aur ads ya profiling ke liye use nahi karte.

AI ko bank password, OTP, Aadhaar ya card number jaisi sensitive cheezein kabhi na likhein ya na dikhayein.

6Suraksha

  • PIN aur security answer SHA-256 hash me save hote hain, seedhe nahi.
  • 5 baar galat PIN par 60 second ka lock lag jaata hai.
  • Cloud se baat HTTPS par hoti hai, aur Google apne servers par data encrypted rakhta hai.
Zaroori baat: khata data end-to-end encrypted nahi hai. Mobile number par OTP verification nahi hota, isliye number aur PIN jis ke paas ho wo account khol sakta hai. PIN kisi ko na batayein, aur shared phone par logout zarur karein.

Koi bhi online system 100% surakshit nahi hota, isliye samay-samay par Backup download karte rahein.

7Aapke paas kya control hai

  • Dekhna aur nikalna: PDF, Excel/CSV aur JSON Backup se apna poora data nikaal sakte hain.
  • Badalna ya hatana: kisi bhi entry ko edit ya delete kar sakte hain (delete par Undo milta hai).
  • PIN badalna: Settings me PIN aur security question badal sakte hain.
  • Account ka cloud data hatwana: developer se request karein.

8Data kitne din rehta hai

Jab tak aap entries delete na karein ya hatane ki request na karein. Logout karne se sirf login hat'ta hai, phone me saved khata ki copy nahi hat'ti. Use hatane ke liye browser ka site data clear karein (Cookies Notice dekhein).

9Udhar wali parties ka data

Udhar khata me aap doosre logon ke naam aur lena-dena likhte hain. Ye aapki zimmedari hai ki aap sirf zaroori jaankari likhein. WhatsApp reminder bhejne se pehle samne wale ka dhyan rakhein.

10Bachche

Ye app 18 saal se kam umr ke bachchon ke liye nahi banaya gaya hai.

11Is policy me badlav

Hum zaroorat padne par ise badal sakte hain. Naya version app me yahin dikhega aur upar likhi tarikh badal jaayegi. App chalate rehna naya version maanna hai.
